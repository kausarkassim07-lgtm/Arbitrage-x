05 / REAL-WORLD CHECK

A mathematical
signal isn't a guarantee.

Real trading includes bid/ask spreads, transaction fees, liquidity limits, slippage and execution delays. A theoretical cycle can disappear before a trade is completed. This project focuses on detection and learning.

06 / QUESTIONS

Good to know.

What is currency arbitrage? ＋

It is the possibility of gaining value through a sequence of currency conversions when the product of the exchange rates in the cycle is greater than 1.

Why use negative logarithms? ＋

Because multiplying rates around a cycle becomes adding their negative logarithms. A product greater than 1 becomes a negative total weight.

Why Floyd–Warshall? ＋

It computes shortest paths between every pair of vertices in O(V³) time and can reveal a negative cycle through a negative diagonal entry.

Does this app execute trades? ＋

No. It is an educational detection prototype. It does not connect to a broker, exchange or trading account.
